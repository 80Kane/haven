The pilot

Is HavenForward open to join?

Not yet. HavenForward is an invitation-only pilot in development. Member enrollment and community conversations are not open, and there is no public account registration or email-interest collection on this site today.

How will people join?

The pilot will begin with invitations after privacy, access controls, and moderation readiness are reviewed. Future invitations will be limited, expiring, and single-use. An interest request will not automatically grant community membership when that service becomes available.

How the pilot will begin

Can I sign up for email updates?

Not at this time. Email-interest registration is not active. Any future email service must request clear consent, verify addresses, provide unsubscribe options, and explain retention before collecting information. Interest registration will be separate from pilot invitations.

What are the community circles?

Seven proposed spaces for the pilot: Recovery & Healing, Choices & Change, Mental Wellness, Veterans Connection, Family & Relationships, Reentry & New Beginnings, and Purpose & Personal Growth. Future access will require an invitation and verified membership.

Safety & support

Is HavenForward therapy or treatment?

No. Peer support is about listening and mutual respect. It does not replace professional care, treatment, or emergency services.

Find treatment and other support resources

What if I need help right now?

HavenForward is not an emergency service and does not monitor this website for crises. In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Outside the US, use local crisis resources.

More free US support lines

How will the community be moderated?

Before launch, the pilot must have reporting, blocking and muting, a staffed review process, documented escalation, limited-access records, and an appeal path. Safety-critical decisions require human oversight. There will be no unrestricted direct messaging in the initial pilot. These tools are not active on the public site.

Privacy

Will I have to share my story or use my real name?

No. Members will use a chosen name, and personal stories should never be a requirement for belonging. You decide whether to participate, what to share, and when to step away.

A chosen name is not a guarantee of anonymity. Email verification, hosting logs, and account recovery may identify a member.

Does this website track me?

The site does not install analytics, advertising trackers, session replay, or application cookies. Resource searches happen in your browser and are not sent to a HavenForward backend. Visiting any website sends technical information, such as your IP address, to its hosting infrastructure.

This website

Are the listed resources partners or endorsements?

No. Listing does not imply a partnership, endorsement, open appointment, or available benefit. Provider details have not yet been independently verified, so confirm directly with each provider.

How can I give feedback about the website?

Use the public issue tracker on GitHub for non-sensitive website suggestions. It is public, and it is not a member reporting or crisis channel. Please do not share personal information, private stories, or crisis requests there.
