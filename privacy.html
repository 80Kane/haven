Privacy & participation

Your information.
Your choices.

This notice describes the public information website as it works today, not a future member application.

Published October 8, 2026.

What this website does

This website provides information and links to external resources. It does not accept email-interest submissions, create member accounts, store journal entries, or operate community conversations. No Hug reaction data is collected. Resource searches happen in your browser and are not sent to a HavenForward backend.

Hosting and technical information

The public site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it involves sending technical information, such as your IP address and requested page, to the hosting infrastructure. HavenForward does not control all hosting logs or provider retention. Read GitHub’s privacy statement for provider practices.

The site does not install analytics, advertising trackers, session replay, or application cookies. Your browser or external providers may store information under their own policies.

External resources and public feedback

Provider websites and GitHub have their own privacy practices. Clicking a resource link leaves HavenForward. Do not post personal stories, medical details, contact information, or crisis requests in public GitHub feedback.

Before the private pilot opens

Enrollment will stay closed until access controls, consent, privacy settings, moderation, recovery, and account removal are tested. A member privacy notice must explain the data collected, who can access it, retention, export, removal, and how to contact the responsible team before anyone enrolls.

A chosen name is not a guarantee of anonymity. Email verification, hosting logs, and account recovery may identify a member. We will not promise owner-only encryption or confidentiality beyond what has been implemented and reviewed.

Email updates

Email-interest registration is not active. Any future email service must request clear consent, verify addresses, provide unsubscribe options, and explain retention before collecting information. Interest registration will be separate from pilot invitations.

Questions about this public site

For non-sensitive website feedback, use the public repository issue tracker. A secure private contact channel must be established before member-data collection begins. Please do not submit private information through public feedback.

Support beyond HavenForward

Need support right now?

In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Outside the US, use local crisis resources.

HavenForward is not an emergency service and does not monitor this website for crises.
