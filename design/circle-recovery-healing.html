Circle agreements — please read before posting
  1. Share only what you choose. No one has to explain their story.
  2. Speak from your own experience; ask before offering advice.
  3. Keep what others share here. No screenshots or outside sharing.
  4. Peer support doesn’t replace professional care or emergency help.
