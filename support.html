If you need help right now

You deserve support, whatever this moment feels like.

HavenForward is a peer-support community, not an emergency service. You can reach out for yourself or someone you care about. You do not have to wait until things feel unbearable.

Immediate danger or medical emergency: Call 911.

Crisis support

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

    Free emotional and crisis support, 24/7. Call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line or 2 for Spanish. Chat is also available.

    Call 988Text 988988 website & chat

  • Crisis Text Line

    Free support by text, 24/7. Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a volunteer crisis counselor.

    Text 741741 — send HOMECrisis Text Line website

  • The Trevor Project

    Free crisis support for LGBTQ+ young people, 24/7. Call, use online chat, or text START to 678-678.

    Call 1-866-488-7386Text 678-678 — send STARTTrevor website & chat

  • Trans Lifeline

    Peer support run by trans people for trans and questioning people. Regular hours: Monday–Friday, noon–8pm Central (1–9pm Eastern). Check the provider website for closures and current availability; this line is not 24/7.

    Call 877-565-8860Trans Lifeline hours & details

Substance use & overdose

  • SAMHSA National Helpline

    Free treatment referrals and information, 24/7, in English and Spanish. This is an information service, not emergency response.

    Call 1-800-662-4357SAMHSA helpline details

  • FindTreatment.gov

    Explore mental health and substance use treatment options. Confirm costs, eligibility and current openings directly with the provider.

    Find treatment options

  • Never Use Alone

    Peer-run overdose response support by phone, 24/7. If you may use substances alone, an operator can stay on the line. They ask for your location and alert emergency services if you stop responding. This does not replace 911 in an emergency.

    Call 800-484-3731Call 877-696-1996Never Use Alone details

  • Poison Control

    Call for guidance about possible poisoning, medication mistakes or substance exposure. If someone collapses, has a seizure, has trouble breathing or cannot be awakened, call 911 immediately.

    Call 1-800-222-1222Poison Control guidance

Information, peer support & basic needs

  • NAMI HelpLine

    Mental health information, support and referrals. Monday–Friday, 10am–10pm Eastern. Text NAMI to 62640. This is not a crisis line; use 988 for crisis support.

    Call 1-800-950-6264Text 62640 — send NAMINAMI hours & details

  • 211

    Connect with local help for housing, food, utilities, transportation and other basic needs. Call 211 or look up your local service. Text and online options vary by location.

    Call 211Find your local 211

Safety from violence or abuse

If someone may monitor your device, consider using a safer device to contact support. Follow the provider’s digital safety guidance.

  • National Domestic Violence Hotline

    Support from a live advocate by phone, text or online chat. Text START to 88788.

    Call 1-800-799-7233Text 88788 — send STARTDomestic violence support & chat

  • RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline

    Free support for survivors of sexual assault and people supporting them, 24/7. Phone and online chat are available.

    Call 1-800-656-4673RAINN support & chat
