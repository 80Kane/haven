Community & safety

Peer voice.
Peer choice.

These principles guide the invitation-only pilot being developed. Member conversations and moderation tools are not open on this website.

Participation is a choice

You decide whether to participate, what to share, and when to step away. Nobody should pressure someone to disclose a story, diagnosis, treatment, recovery milestone, or personal details.

Listen with respect

Speak from your own experience. Ask before offering advice. Respect different paths, identities, boundaries, and beliefs. Peer support does not replace professional care.

Protect privacy

Do not share another person’s identity, story, screenshots, or contact information without permission. Do not solicit identifying information. Respect that a chosen name does not guarantee anonymity.

Keep the community welcoming

Harassment, threats, discrimination, exploitation, doxxing, scams, and pressure to enter private contact are not acceptable. There will be no unrestricted direct messaging in the initial pilot.

Human moderation and understandable decisions

Before launch, the pilot must have reporting, blocking and muting, a staffed review process, documented escalation, limited-access records, and an appeal path. Safety-critical decisions require human oversight. These tools are not active on the public site.

Member notices must explain actions and how to appeal while protecting reporters’ privacy. A private concern-reporting channel and moderator availability must be published before enrollment opens.

When immediate help is needed

HavenForward is not an emergency service. It does not promise continuous monitoring, immediate responses, or emergency dispatch. In the US, call 988 or text 988. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Outside the US, use local crisis resources.

Help shape the public website

Website feedback on GitHub is public and suitable only for non-sensitive website suggestions. It is not a member reporting or crisis channel.

Support beyond HavenForward

Need support right now?

In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Outside the US, use local crisis resources.

HavenForward is not an emergency service and does not monitor this website for crises.
