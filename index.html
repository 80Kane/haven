Our purpose

A place to connect.
Space to be yourself.

Moving forward looks different for everyone. You don’t have to have it all figured out to seek connection.

HavenForward is developing a moderated peer-support community for recovery, personal growth, education, resources, and meaningful social connection. Lived experience matters here. Participation is voluntary, and sharing is always a choice.

Peer support is about listening and mutual respect. It does not replace professional care, treatment, or emergency services.

The invitation-only pilot

A thoughtful beginning.
Built around people.

Member enrollment is not open yet. The pilot will begin with invitations after privacy, access controls, and moderation readiness are reviewed.

There is no public account registration or email-interest collection on this site today. An interest request will not automatically grant community membership when that service becomes available.

Support beyond HavenForward

Need support right now?

In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Outside the US, use local crisis resources.

HavenForward is not an emergency service and does not monitor this website for crises.
