Our purpose

A place to connect.
Space to be yourself.

Moving forward looks different for everyone. You don’t have to have it all figured out to seek connection.

HavenForward is developing a moderated peer-support community for recovery, personal growth, education, resources, and meaningful social connection. Lived experience matters here. Participation is voluntary, and sharing is always a choice.

Peer support is about listening and mutual respect. It does not replace professional care, treatment, or emergency services.

Community circles

Different journeys.
Shared connection.

These are proposed spaces for the pilot. Future access will require an invitation and verified membership.

  • Recovery & Healing

    A space for different recovery journeys, shared experience, and support at your own pace.

  • Choices & Change

    Explore decisions and new directions without pressure to follow someone else’s path.

  • Mental Wellness

    Make room for everyday well-being, difficult days, and the practices that help.

  • Veterans Connection

    Connection through lived experience, service, transitions, and life beyond the uniform.

  • Family & Relationships

    Reflect on relationships, boundaries, caregiving, and the people who matter to you.

  • Reentry & New Beginnings

    Support for returning to community, rebuilding connections, and finding your next step.

  • Purpose & Personal Growth

    Explore learning, creativity, meaningful goals, and your own definition of moving forward.

  • Your experience matters

    There’s no single way forward.

    The pilot’s spaces will grow through peer feedback and human moderation.

Built with care

Support without pressure.

We’re starting small so the community can grow thoughtfully.

  • You choose what to share

    Members will use a chosen name. Personal stories should never be a requirement for belonging.

  • People guide the community

    Human review, understandable guidelines, and a way to raise concerns are requirements for the pilot.

  • Small gestures matter

    Hug reactions are planned for the community. They are not active here, and we do not display invented engagement counts.

The invitation-only pilot

A thoughtful beginning.
Built around people.

Member enrollment is not open yet. The pilot will begin with invitations after privacy, access controls, and moderation readiness are reviewed.

An interest request will not automatically grant community membership when that service becomes available.

Understand privacy & participation

  1. A personal invitation

    Future invitations will be limited, expiring, and single-use.

  2. Informed participation

    Read the guidelines and privacy information, then decide whether to join.

  3. Connection at your pace

    Choose your spaces and what you share. Participation is voluntary.
