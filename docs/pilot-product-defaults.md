# Pilot product defaults

Tim approved these defaults in the HavenForward project conversation on
2026-11-14. They guide implementation; they do not imply every feature is live.

- Hugs: private recipient notification, optional notifications, no public counts
  in the member pilot.
- Moderation: chosen staff name and published coverage hours. Show on-duty status
  only when availability is verified. Moderation is not emergency response.
- Directory: unlisted by default; listing requires an explicit choice and can be
  withdrawn.
- Posting: optional content notes and an author option to turn replies off.
  Reporting and moderator review remain available.
- Identity: pseudonyms are welcome, with a unique handle and reserved staff
  labels. Display-name changes have a reasonable cooldown. The first settings
  implementation uses seven days; privacy preferences remain editable anytime.
- Learning: publish only material owned by the uploader or covered by documented
  permission, including separate K.E.Y.S. and CEO Hockley permission checks.
  Content begins as private drafts. Support captioned video, audio/transcripts,
  and accessible PDF worksheets.
- Support: distinguish emotional-distress support (call/text 988) from immediate
  danger or medical emergencies (911), with compact access on every screen.

Implement settings before opening member conversations. FAQ/support-line changes
need a concrete PR review; approval of these defaults is not evidence that any
particular support-line wording, staffing claim, or content license is verified.
