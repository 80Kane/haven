# Get help now: resource review

Reviewed 2026-11-14 against the provider pages linked from `support.html`.
This public page extends the support contacts and FAQ merged in PR #35.

## Editorial decisions

- Keep the user's welcoming peer-support language and separate emotional distress (988) from immediate danger or medical emergency (911).
- Describe US coverage explicitly. Do not promise all services are free, always available, or subject to identical confidentiality rules. Providers explain their own privacy limits; phone/data charges may apply.
- Trans Lifeline publishes weekday hours and scheduled closures. It belongs under “Crisis support,” not a heading claiming every entry operates 24/7.
- 211 text services vary locally; publish the national call number and local-service finder instead of promising nationwide texting.
- Never Use Alone lists both numbers and explains location collection and EMS activation. Do not imply it replaces emergency response.
- Poison Control's emergency warning covers collapse, seizures, breathing difficulty and inability to awaken.
- HeyPeers includes free and paid meetings. Avoid describing all meetings as free or every facilitator as certified.
- Do not promise moderator messaging or crisis monitoring. Those capabilities and response staffing are not established by this change.
- Omit the supplied December 11 funding deadline and predicted January service pauses. They were not verified and are unnecessary for this evergreen page.
- Keep moderator-only placement, escalation and follow-up notes out of public content. A crisis response protocol needs separate operational review before automated flagged-post replies or private follow-up are offered.

## Maintenance and deployment

Review provider contacts and hours at least quarterly and whenever a correction is reported; this document does not create an automated review. Preserve the visible review date and update it only after checking details again. The general resource directory's separate unverified status is unchanged.

Publish the public static site with `/support` before checking the member application's new external support links. Authentication, database policies and public interactions are unchanged. The page works without JavaScript; no form, tracking or member information is added.
